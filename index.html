1. Overview

InvoiceWise: Invoice Maker ("the App") is developed by Huebugs. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles your information.

Summary: InvoiceWise works 100% offline. All your data stays on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

2. Data We Collect

We collect no personal data. The App does not require an account, does not transmit data to our servers, and does not use analytics or tracking tools.

All invoice data, client details, and settings you enter are stored locally on your device only, using the App's private storage.

3. Camera Permission

The App requests camera access solely for the receipt scanner feature, which lets you photograph paper receipts to extract text. Images are processed on-device and are never uploaded or shared.

4. Data Sharing

We do not share any data with third parties because we do not collect any data.

5. In-App Purchases

The App offers a one-time in-app purchase (InvoiceWise Pro) processed entirely by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment information. Google's privacy policy applies to the purchase transaction.

6. Data Security

Your data is stored in the App's private internal storage on your device, protected by Android's app sandboxing. Uninstalling the App permanently deletes all local data.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is a general business tool not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date at the top will reflect the latest revision.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at support@huebugs.com.
